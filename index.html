Built for Australia

Australian import rules, already in the maths.

Every official charge comes from the published rule and is labelled, as are estimates and your own figures, so you know exactly what to check.

  • Customs duty · ABF5% or 0%On the FOB value. 0% for Japanese-built cars with proof of origin.
  • Import GST · ABF10%Of the value of the taxable importation: FOB, duty, freight and insurance.
  • Luxury car tax · ATO33%Above A$80,809, or A$91,661 for fuel-efficient cars, in 2026–27.
  • Motor vehicle duty · VICA$8.40–A$18Per A$200 of value, in bands at A$80,809, A$100,000 and A$150,000.
  • Registration checksRAV and VASSFlags cars not yet on the RAV, modified or converted cars and cars over 15 years old.

Photo studio

Studio listing photos, shot on a phone.

Your team walks round the car with the AutoPivot app. AutoPivot puts it on your backdrop, evens the lighting and blurs the plate, and each photo appears on the listing when it's ready.

  • Guided capture

    An outline and a level line up eight angles, and every shot is checked for blur, glare and a cut-off car.

  • Automatic processing

    The car is cut out and placed on your backdrop with realistic tyre contact and shadow. The plate is blurred, pixelated or covered.

  • Tidy listings

    Every car gets a listing with its details and photos. Redo one shot, keep an original, add photos later or archive it when it sells.

  • Built for teams

    Administrators add staff and reset passwords. Everyone sees the same listings, and the app locks with Face ID or a fingerprint.

Questions

What dealers ask first

Does AutoPivot bid on or buy cars for me?

No. It finds cars, reads them and costs them. You review every estimate and place bids yourself. Condition, import eligibility, final costs and every bid stay with you.

Which state does the driveaway cost cover?

Victoria: motor vehicle duty, roadworthy, registration, TAC and plates. Tell us your state when you get in touch.

Where do the resale prices come from?

Matching Australian listings from Autotrader, plus sales records you import. Each estimate shows how many comparables it used, a low to high range and a confidence score with its reasons.

How accurate are the costs?

Duty, GST, luxury car tax and Victorian duty follow published rules. Freight, insurance, compliance and repairs are estimates you can change. Every line is labelled, so treat the result as decision support and confirm final costs with quotes.

Why do some auction cars show no profit yet?

Auction lots often have no price until they sell. An opening bid isn't a price, so AutoPivot waits for one. Enter the price you'd pay to see the full estimate.

Do we need a studio or a special camera?

No. The AutoPivot app runs on iPhone and Android phones. It guides each angle on screen and puts the car on your dealership's backdrop for you.

Who can use the photo app?

Your dealership's administrator creates staff accounts, so there's no public sign-up. Everyone in the dealership sees the same listings, whoever took the photos.

What happens to number plates?

AutoPivot finds the plate in every photo and blurs it. It can also pixelate the plate or cover it instead.

Can I use my own costs?

Yes. Dealership cost settings cover agent fees, freight, insurance, duty overrides and on-road costs. New searches use them, and each car keeps the settings it was costed with.
