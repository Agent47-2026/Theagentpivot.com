What AutoPivot does

Import search, in the web portal

Finds cars in Japan worth importing, works out what each one costs to put on the road in Victoria, compares it with Australian resale prices and shows the profit. Every costed car also shows its max bid: the most you can pay in Japan and still make the profit you want.

Photo studio, in the phone app

  • Guided capture. The app walks you round the vehicle one angle at a time, with an outline for each shot, a level guide, and checks for blur, glare and a car that's cut off.
  • Automatic processing. Photos go to the AutoPivot server, which finds the vehicle, places it on your dealership's backdrop, evens out the lighting and blurs the number plate.
  • Listings. Each vehicle gets a listing with its details and finished photos. Redo a shot, keep an original as it is, or add more photos later.
  • Your team. Dealership administrators add and deactivate staff and reset passwords.

Getting started

  1. Get an account from your dealership. AutoPivot accounts are created by your dealership's administrator, who gives you a one-time password.
  2. Sign in and choose a new password. You're asked to replace the one-time password the first time you sign in.
  3. Start a listing. Tap the camera button, then follow the outline for each angle.
  4. Review and send. Retake anything you're not happy with, then send the set for processing. The finished photos appear on the listing.

Tips for great photos

  • Keep the whole vehicle inside the outline, with a little space around it.
  • Hold the phone level at about waist height, and hold still until the shutter is ready.
  • Avoid strong reflections and direct sun on the lens.

Common questions

Can I sign up on my own?

No. AutoPivot is a tool for dealership teams, so accounts are created by your dealership's administrator. If your dealership would like to use AutoPivot, contact us.

I forgot my password.

Ask your dealership administrator to reset it from the Team page. You'll get a new one-time password and choose your own when you next sign in.

How long does processing take?

Usually a few minutes per vehicle, and longer at busy times. You can keep using the app; a banner shows when photos are still processing.

Is there a demo?

Yes. Turn on demo mode in Settings to try the capture flow with sample photos.

Are the costs and profits a quote?

No. They are estimates to support your decisions, built from official rates where they exist and clearly marked estimates where they don't. Check the figures with your agent, broker and compliance workshop before you buy.

Privacy

AutoPivot keeps your work account, your listings and their photos on our server in Australia so your team can use them. No ads and no tracking. Read the full AutoPivot privacy policy.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete account in the app, or see how to delete your account for other options.
