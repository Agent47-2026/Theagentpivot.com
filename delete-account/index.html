Your account

Delete your AutoPivot account

AutoPivot is made by TheAgent47.

In the app

  1. Open AutoPivot and sign in.
  2. Tap your account at the top of the screen, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out on every device.

Through our website

If you can't sign in, use the form on our home page. Enter your work email address and write "Delete my AutoPivot account" in the message box. We'll confirm it's you and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and password are erased.
  • Your account is closed and every session is signed out.

What is kept

  • Listings and photos you created belong to your dealership and stay with it. Your dealership's administrator can delete them; deleting a listing removes its photos from our server.
  • The activity record keeps an entry that an account was deleted, without your name or email address, for security.

Dealership administrators: if you're your dealership's only administrator, add another administrator on the Team page first, or contact us to close the whole dealership account. We delete a closed dealership's listings, photos and accounts within 30 days.
