Privacy

AutoPivot Privacy Policy

AutoPivot is made and run by TheAgent47 (theagent47.com). On Google Play and the Apple App Store the app (com.luxtradita.autopivot) is currently published under the developer account of Lux Tradita, on TheAgent47's behalf.
Contact: the form on theautopivot.com · Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version: AutoPivot is a work tool for car dealerships. It keeps your work account, and the vehicle listings and photos your team creates, on our server in Australia so your team can use them. No ads, no tracking, no selling data.

Who this covers

This policy covers the AutoPivot phone app, the AutoPivot web portal and the AutoPivot service they connect to, and the contact form on theautopivot.com. Accounts are created by a dealership's administrator for its staff; the dealership decides what its team photographs, lists and searches for.

What we collect and why

Photos can show whatever was in the frame when they were taken. Please photograph vehicles, not people.

What stays on your device

  • Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage, or your browser for the web portal, so you stay signed in.
  • Photos you haven't sent yet are kept as a draft on your phone until you send or discard them.
  • The live camera view is checked on the phone for a vehicle in frame, blur and glare while you capture. These checks run on the phone; the live view is not stored or sent.
  • Motion sensor readings are used on the phone to show the level guide. They are not stored or sent.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint unlock are handled by your phone's operating system. AutoPivot only learns whether the check passed; it never receives your biometric data.
  • App settings (such as haptics, app lock and demo mode) are stored on your phone. Web portal preferences (such as the profit you want from a car, and comparable sales you add yourself) are stored in your browser.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to photograph vehicles. Only used while you're capturing.
  • Motion (iPhone): for the level guide while capturing.
  • Face ID / biometrics: only if you turn on app lock.

AutoPivot does not use your location, contacts or microphone.

Where your data is processed

The AutoPivot server runs on Fly.io in Sydney, Australia. Photos are processed on that server by models we run ourselves; we don't send your photos, your listings or your account details to third-party AI services. Data travels between the app, the web portal and the server over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Import search reads public car adverts on Japanese and Australian websites. To fetch and read them, our server sends those adverts' web addresses to Exa and their text to an AI model through OpenRouter. These are public car adverts; nothing about you, your team or your customers is sent with them.

Messages sent through the theautopivot.com contact form are delivered to us by email through Web3Forms.

Who can see it

  • People in your dealership with an AutoPivot account can see your dealership's listings, photos and import searches. Administrators can also see and manage the team list.
  • AutoPivot platform administrators at TheAgent47 can see dealership accounts and activity to run and support the service.
  • Our hosting provider stores the data on our behalf.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We'd only disclose it if the law required us to.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.
  • No public sign-up: only dealership administrators create accounts.

How long we keep it

  • Listings and photos are kept until your dealership deletes them. Deleting a listing removes its photos from our server.
  • Your account is kept until you delete it, or your administrator deactivates it.
  • When a dealership stops using AutoPivot and asks us to close its account, we delete its listings, photos, import searches and user accounts within 30 days.
  • Contact form messages are kept only as long as we need them to reply and to keep a record of what was agreed.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete account in the app and confirm with your password. This erases your name, email address and password and signs you out everywhere. Listings you created belong to your dealership and stay with it. You can also ask us through our website; see how to delete your account.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us through the form on theautopivot.com with your work email address. We'll reply within 30 days. If your request is about your dealership's listings, we may need to involve your dealership's administrator. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children

AutoPivot is a business tool for dealership staff. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
